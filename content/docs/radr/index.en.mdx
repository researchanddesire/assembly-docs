## Safety first

RADR contains a 3.7 V 800 mAh lithium polymer (LiPo) battery, which stays
connected for most of the build. The build also uses a rasp, isopropyl alcohol,
a razor blade, and a hot glue gun. Read the safety notes at each step of the
[assembly guide](/radr/assembly-guide).

- If a battery swells, leaks, gets hot, or smells, stop charging or using it
  and move it to a non-flammable surface, away from anything that can burn. Do
  not use it again.
- [Battery & charging](https://docs.researchanddesire.com/radr/faqs#battery--charging):
  charge the finished remote through its USB-C port.
- RADR controls other devices. Before you use it with an OSSM, follow the
  [OSSM Pre-play Safety Checklist](https://docs.researchanddesire.com/ossm/how-to-use#pre-play-safety-checklist).

## Product Overview

RADR is a wireless remote built around a populated RADR PCB assembly, 2.0 inch SPI display, 800 mAh LiPo battery, printed front and back housings, printed buttons, printed power switch cover, printed rotary knob, weather stripping, and tri-lobe enclosure screws.

## Product Dimensions and Materials

Known material and part notes:

- Housing: FDM-printed RADR front and back housing.
- Display: 2.0 inch 14-pin SPI LCD display.
- Battery: 3.7 V 800 mAh LiPo battery.
- Fasteners: `BC-0408LTFB` tri-lobe screws.
- Cushioning: 2.5 cm piece of weather stripping installed on the PCBA.
