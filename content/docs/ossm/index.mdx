## OSSM assembly

Start with [Before You Build](/ossm/build/introduction), then follow the stages in order. Do not apply power until the assembly is secured and the first-test instructions tell you to do so.

The guide covers:

- the bill of materials and standard printed parts;
- rail, belt, tensioner, end-effector, and motorhead assembly;
- control-board mounting and official wiring;
- homing and a low-speed first test.

For PCB, protocol, firmware, and integration details, visit [Developer Docs](https://dev.researchanddesire.com/ossm).
