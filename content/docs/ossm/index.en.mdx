The OSSM repository is being rebuilt around new hardware sources.

The [motor control harness](/ossm/cable-harnesses) is the first preserved design.
Mechanical CAD, PCB designs, and machine assembly instructions will be added as
the new designs become available.

## Safety first

The OSSM runs from a 24 V DC power supply that plugs into mains power, and its
motor moves the carriage along the rail with enough force to cause injury.

- Make wiring changes only while the power supply is unplugged from the wall.
- Use the 24 V power supply listed in the [bill of materials](/ossm/bom).
  Third-party supplies can damage the controller or fail to deliver enough
  power. Never open the supply or modify its mains cable.
- Keep hands, hair, and loose objects clear of the rail when you apply power.
  The OSSM starts homing automatically and drives the carriage to both ends of
  its travel.
- Before using a finished machine, follow
  [Connecting your device to power](https://docs.researchanddesire.com/ossm/how-to-use#connecting-your-device-to-power)
  and the
  [Pre-play Safety Checklist](https://docs.researchanddesire.com/ossm/how-to-use#pre-play-safety-checklist).
