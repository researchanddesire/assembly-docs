# Stop before troubleshooting

Disconnect power and wait for all movement to stop before touching the belt, motor, carriage, wiring, or control board.

## The carriage binds by hand

Recheck rail alignment, belt routing, bearing alignment, the front hard limit, and belt tension. Return to the stage where movement first became restricted.

## The control board does not power on

Disconnect power. Verify polarity and terminal placement against [Wiring](/ossm/build/wiring). Inspect for loose strands or exposed conductor. Do not repeatedly apply power to uncertain wiring.

## Homing moves in the wrong direction

Disconnect power immediately and recheck the motor direction and pulse wiring. Do not continue to a motion test.

## The belt skips or walks sideways

Disconnect power and review [Pulley on Motor](/ossm/build/pulley) and [Belt Tension](/ossm/build/belt-tension). The pulley, belt, and bearings must stay aligned through the full hand-checked travel.

If the checks do not identify the cause, contact [support@researchanddesire.com](mailto:support@researchanddesire.com) before applying power again.
