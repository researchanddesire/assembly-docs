# Purchased components

This list is for the official standard configuration described by this guide.

| Component | Standard specification | Quantity |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Integrated servo motor | NEMA 23, 57AIM30 class, 100 W | 1 |
| OSSM control board | Official 24 V OSSM PCB | 1 |
| Controller | Official wired remote or RADR | 1 |
| Timing pulley | GT2, 20 tooth, 8 mm bore, for 10 mm belt | 1 |
| Timing belt | GT2, 10 mm wide, at least 500 mm long | 1 |
| Bearings | MR115, 5 × 11 × 4 mm | 6 |
| Power supply | Safety-certified, fully enclosed, 24 V DC, 5 A | 1 |
| Linear rail | 350 mm rail with carriage | 1 |
| Metric hardware | M3 and M5 bolts, nuts, and washers listed by each stage | 1 set |
| Motor wiring | Official power and four-pin data cables | 1 set |

<Warning>
  Use a safety-certified power supply appropriate for your region. The power supply must be fully enclosed; do not leave mains terminals exposed.
</Warning>

For connector limits and board details, use the [OSSM developer reference](https://dev.researchanddesire.com/ossm/hardware/pcb/introduction).

<Check>
  Confirm the motor, control board, power supply, pulley bore, belt width, and rail length before opening or assembling the printed parts.
</Check>
