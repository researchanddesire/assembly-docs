---
title: Cable Harnesses
---

The DT Trainer build includes a screen, sensor, battery, and PCB cable harness
as a top-level assembly item in the [Bill of Materials](/dtt/bom).

No Wireviz harness source has been migrated to `hardware/cables/` yet. When a
Wireviz source is added, keep the product-level BOM focused on the harness
assembly and link detailed child BOM output from this page.

| Asset | Location | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cable source folder | [`hardware/cables/`](https://github.com/researchanddesire/DT_Trainer-OSS/tree/main/hardware/cables/) | Wireviz YAML sources belong here. |
| Product-level BOM | [`hardware/bom.csv`](https://github.com/researchanddesire/DT_Trainer-OSS/blob/main/hardware/bom.csv) | Lists the cable harness as an assembly line item. |
