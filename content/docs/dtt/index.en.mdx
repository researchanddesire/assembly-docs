## Safety first

The DTT contains a lithium polymer (LiPo) battery. The build uses a torch and a
hot glue gun inside the printed housing. Read the safety notes at each step of
the [assembly guide](/dtt/assembly-guide).

- If a battery swells, leaks, gets hot, or smells, stop charging or using it.
  If it is safe to do so, move it to a non-flammable surface, away from
  anything that can burn. Do not use it again. If it smokes or catches fire,
  leave the area and call emergency services.
- [Charging your battery](https://docs.researchanddesire.com/dtt/specs/charging):
  charge with dry, undamaged cables and adapters.
- [Before you begin](https://docs.researchanddesire.com/dtt/quick-start/getting-started#before-you-begin):
  mount the finished Trainer only on smooth, non-porous surfaces, and stop
  immediately if you feel pain or discomfort.
